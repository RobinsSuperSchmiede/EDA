OpenShell runs apps that you install on your device. OpenShell itself does not collect, transmit
or sell personal data, and it has no servers that receive data from the app.

- **Data on your device.** Apps you add and their data (databases, files, logs) are stored in
  OpenShell's private storage on your device. Uninstalling OpenShell or removing an app deletes them.
- **Network.** An app's user interface talks to its own services on your device (127.0.0.1). If you
  switch on remote access for an app, your other devices can reach it over your network with a
  token you create; nothing is sent anywhere else.
- **Notifications.** A notification shows which app is running. It is not used for anything else.
- **Third-party apps.** Apps you install may process data themselves. Their own policies apply.
- **Children.** OpenShell is not directed at children.
