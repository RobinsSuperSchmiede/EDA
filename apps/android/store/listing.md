Copy these texts into the Play Console (*Grow → Store presence → Main store listing*).

## App name (max. 30 characters)

OpenShell – apps on your phone

## Short description (max. 80 characters)

Run your own web apps, databases and AI functions locally on your phone.

## Full description (max. 4000 characters)

OpenShell turns your phone into a personal app host.

Apps built with OpenShell – on a computer, in Termux or by an AI agent – run here unchanged:
their user interface, their built-in SQLite database and their functions stay on your phone.
Nothing is uploaded to a server.

• Add apps as .osapp files: open or share them with OpenShell
• Each app keeps its own private data, also across updates
• Apps keep running in the background while you need them (a notification shows what runs)
• Optional: let your other devices use an app over your Wi-Fi or VPN – off by default, protected
  by per-device tokens, an address allowlist, rate limits and TLS
• Made for apps that AI agents build and use through the Model Context Protocol (MCP)

OpenShell is open source (MIT): https://github.com/RobinsSuperSchmiede/EDA
