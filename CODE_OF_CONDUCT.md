# Code of Conduct

OpenShell follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep discussions focused on the
work. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and publishing others' private information are not tolerated –
in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers via a
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/RobinsSuperSchmiede/EDA/security/advisories/new) (it is private) or by
contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content and restrict or
ban participants who violate this code, following the Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines.
